Thoughts

A free-form canvas for scattered ideas — no folders, no lists, just space to think.

Holoholo

A post-Arc browser for macOS — native, written in Swift and powered by Chromium. It is quick, modern and carefully thought through: tabs live in a sidebar and are organized into Spaces. Websites behave as they do in Chrome, and extensions from the Chrome Web Store just work.
